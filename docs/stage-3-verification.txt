2. Multi-cloud portability and certification statements
-------------------------------------------------------
Both documents state, in their own words, that the IaC and container
configuration are cloud-agnostic (AWS EC2 + EBS + VPC, GCP GCE + Persistent
Disk + VPC, OCI A1.Flex + Block Volume + VCN), and that final production
verification and testing certification are performed strictly on Oracle Cloud
Infrastructure with trial credits, high-RAM Ampere ARM instances and persistent
block storage. AWS and GCP are documented ports, not certified targets.
